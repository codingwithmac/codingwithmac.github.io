01. About Me

I am a Python developer based in Beaumont with a background in IT that gave me a deep appreciation for automation and scripting. It started with PowerShell — I loved the idea that a few lines of code could eliminate hours of manual work.

When I discovered Python, that passion grew into something bigger. The language is remarkably powerful and I have used it to build tools that solve problems I was personally frustrated with — from tracking prices on items I buy regularly, to organizing cluttered file systems, to generating secure passwords on demand.

I am looking to bring that same problem-first thinking to a team that is passionate about using technology to make a real difference.
